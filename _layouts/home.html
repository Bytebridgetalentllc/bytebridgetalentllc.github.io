The company

ByteBridge Talent LLC is a technology talent and IT staffing company headquartered in Grand Island, Nebraska. It was founded in February 2025. Sixteen people make up the team.

The work is to source engineers, screen them, and place them with the organizations that will employ them. Technical screening is set up around each client's stack, so the review follows the tools and systems that team already runs.

What we staff

IT staff augmentation puts engineers onto a team that is already in place. Cloud and DevOps engineering covers CI/CD, infrastructure as code, containers and orchestration, monitoring, and cloud cost and security hygiene. Software development talent covers backend, frontend, and full-stack developers, along with data engineers.

How an engagement can run

Contract keeps someone on a defined assignment. Contract-to-hire starts with a few months on contract and then converts to full-time. Direct hire is a full-time placement from the first day.

Working with clients directly

ByteBridge Talent LLC contracts with client organizations itself. Medica Health Plans is the anchor direct client. The company is a direct vendor there and has placed 60+ technology professionals over about two years, in software, cloud, and data roles.
