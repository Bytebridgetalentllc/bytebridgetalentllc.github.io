Grand Island, Nebraska is the headquarters of ByteBridge Talent LLC, a technology talent and IT staffing company founded in February 2025. The team is 16 people. Public information about the business is at [bytebridgetalent.com](https://bytebridgetalent.com).

## Services

### IT staff augmentation

Staff augmentation is how a client adds engineers to the group they already have, for as long as the work requires.

### Cloud and DevOps engineering

These placements cover CI/CD, infrastructure as code, containers and orchestration, monitoring, and the ongoing hygiene of cloud cost and security.

### Software development talent

The software roles are backend, frontend, and full-stack development, plus data engineers.

### Technical screening

Before a placement, candidates go through technical screening shaped by that client's stack. The point is to test against the platforms the team uses, not a generic checklist.

## Engagement models

### Contract

A contract engagement is a placement for a defined assignment.

### Contract-to-hire

Contract-to-hire runs for a few months on contract, then converts to a full-time role.

### Direct hire

Direct hire places a person into a full-time role at the start of the engagement.

## Direct clients

ByteBridge Talent LLC works with client organizations as their vendor. Medica Health Plans is the anchor direct client. As a direct vendor, the company has placed 60+ technology professionals at Medica Health Plans over about two years, across software, cloud, and data roles.
